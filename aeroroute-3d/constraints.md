# AeroRoute 3D — Constraints

Writing and product guardrails for case study copy, slides, and demo UI.

## System claims — do not

- Do not claim **real-time flight control** or live autopilot integration.
- Do not claim **real-time thermal updraft detection**.
- Do not claim access to **real commercial vertiport** or operator proprietary data.

## Terminology — use

- **Thermal susceptibility proxy** (e.g. historical LST anomaly), not live thermal sensing.
- **Pre-operation route assessment** before pilot operation, not in-flight operations tooling.
- **Synthetic candidate landing nodes** for PoC route-pair evaluation, with clear data status.

## Scope framing

- Portfolio **case study** and route-readiness **sandbox**, not a production ops platform.
- MVP focuses on **whether a candidate corridor is feasible to evaluate** before launch, not certifying flights.

## Claims to avoid

- Exaggerated **safety guarantees** or regulatory approval implications.
- Unsubstantiated **battery-saving** or range guarantees from the proxy layers alone.
- Language that implies **authoritative** no-fly or airspace clearance without human review.

## Tone

- Information-dense, evidence-aware, honest about synthetic vs evidence-based layers (see `data/data-boundary.md`).
